About Us

A Place Where Children Thrive & Families Trust

Hope Day Care is a registered Early Childhood Development centre in Soshanguve, Pretoria. We provide a nurturing, stimulating, and safe environment where every child feels valued and loved.

Our qualified staff follow the CAPs curriculum ensuring your child is school-ready while enjoying every moment of their early years. We open at 5am so working parents have complete peace of mind.
